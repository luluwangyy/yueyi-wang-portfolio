Audiovisual

Type Creative Coding, Sound Synthesis & Lighting Design
Role Creative Technologist / TouchDesigner Artist
Tools TouchDesigner · Adobe Premiere Pro

Collaborated with pianist Jixue Yang and composer Ya-Lan Chen to carry out a live audiovisual performance at Ades Performance Space (New York), Trinity Episcopal Church (Houston), and Indiana State University (Terre Haute). The piece explores meditation's tranquil energy through sound and image, creating an experience that hovers between stillness and struggle. Visually, a fish drifts through inky, swirling water—evoking a quiet tension between meditative calm and entrapment. Through semi-transparent gauze and layered projections, the pianist becomes suspended between the fish and its shadow, woven into the meditation itself.

This piece is another collaboration with pianist Jixue Yang and composer Huijuan. I used TouchDesigner to create an audio-reactive visual based on the composer's electronic MIDI, visualizing the inner tension of intergenerational conflict that the artists wanted to convey in the piece.
