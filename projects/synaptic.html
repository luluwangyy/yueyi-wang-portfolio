Synaptic

A multilayered light box installation that visualizes the energy transmission of synapses.

Type LED Installation
Role Creative Technologist
Duration 2025
Tools Arduino · LED Matrix · Depth-Net AI Image Generation · Silkscreen Printing · Acrylic & Wood Fabrication

Synaptic visualizes the invisible tension and firing of synapses—both biological and artificial—in a three-dimensional, tangible space. By driving dynamic LED patterns through layers of silkscreen-printed acrylic, the installation simulates energy moving through complex networks.
