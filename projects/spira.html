Spira

An interactive astrology atlas

Spira turns a birth chart into a personal, visual experience. Visitors can explore planetary placements, ask questions about their chart, and listen to a short, poetic reading.

My Role

Product designer and creative lead, directing the visual identity, interaction design, AI voice, and frontend development through iterative prototyping.

Tech Stack

React, TypeScript, Vite, SVG/CSS, Astronomy Engine, OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Upstash Redis, and Vercel.

Key Design and Engineering Moments

01

Translating art into interaction

Inspired by Hilma af Klint, I developed a palette of charcoal, ivory, and rainbow bands, paired with Junicode typography and aged-paper textures. Four-point stars reveal planetary symbols on interaction.

02

Making a complex chart approachable

I removed decorative copy, clarified the hierarchy, and adapted the experience for touch: compact summaries, direct line-to-chat interactions, and a shorter mobile transition.

03

Giving AI a specific voice

I shaped the opening reading into two brief paragraphs: an encouraging observation followed by a gentle caution. Follow-up answers stay concise and grounded in calculated placements.

04

Connecting speech and text

ElevenLabs narration drives the text reveal through speech timestamps, keeping pauses, playback, and replay synchronized.

05

Building within constraints

Server-side credentials and persistent usage controls protect the API budget. Visitors can also save their chart as a textured PNG.

Outcome

A deployed experience that brings chart exploration, conversational interpretation, and narration together across desktop and mobile.

Visitor analytics are enabled; usability and engagement outcomes have not yet been measured.
