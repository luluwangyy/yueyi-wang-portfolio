UrbanSky

Type Full-Stack Web Development / Interactive Interfaces
Role Web Engineer, Columbia Imaging and Vision Lab (CAVE)
Duration Mar 2025 – May 2025

Developed a full-stack web application featuring interactive 360° panoramic interfaces to explore a massive dataset of high-dynamic-range (HDR) lighting environments. The platform allows users to navigate over 1,000 HDR environment maps captured across New York City using 360° cameras and pyranometers. I built the interface to help researchers easily access data filtered by GPS location, time of day, and weather conditions to advance studies in solar energy and computer graphics.
