About

Code & + X ~ Craft

Yueyi Wang is an engineer and artist building interactive systems where creative ideas meet cutting-edge technology. As a recent graduate of Columbia University with a double major in Computer Science and Visual Arts, she creates work that bridges technical precision and creative expression — whether that involves generative AI tools that visualize music or robotics that move in response to an audience's clap.

She recently completed an interactive robotic installation as a 2025–26 Student Artist-in-Residence at the Barnard Movement Lab. In addition to her independent projects, she has contributed her technical and creative skills to the Computational Design Lab, directed by Dr. Lydia Chilton; the Imaging and Vision Laboratory, directed by Dr. Shree Nayar; and the Design Tool Lab, directed by Dr. Tiffany Tseng.
