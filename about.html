Hi, I'm Yueyi Lulu Wang. I design and prototype interactive experiences across generative AI, embodied AI, and digital-physical environments. As a recent graduate of Columbia University with a double major in Computer Science and Visual Arts, she combines technical precision with creative experimentation to explore new forms of interaction.

Her experience spans the Computational Design Lab, directed by Dr. Lydia Chilton; the Imaging and Vision Laboratory, directed by Dr. Shree Nayar; and the Design Tool Lab, directed by Dr. Tiffany Tseng. Beyond her work and research in software and interface design, she is particularly interested in embodied AI and intelligent systems. Most recently, she completed an interactive robotic installation as a 2025–26 Student Artist-in-Residence at the Barnard Movement Lab.
