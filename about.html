Hi, I'm Yueyi Lulu Wang. I'm a design engineer and creative technologist who creates interactive experiences across generative AI, embodied AI, and digital-physical environments.

My experience spans the Computational Design Lab, directed by Dr. Lydia Chilton; the Imaging and Vision Laboratory, directed by Dr. Shree Nayar; and the Design Tool Lab, directed by Dr. Tiffany Tseng. Beyond my work and research in software and interface design, I am particularly interested in embodied AI and intelligent systems. Most recently, I completed an interactive robotic installation as a 2025–26 Student Artist-in-Residence at the Barnard Movement Lab.

As a recent graduate of Columbia University with a double major in Computer Science and Visual Arts, I combine technical precision with creative experimentation to explore new forms of interaction.
